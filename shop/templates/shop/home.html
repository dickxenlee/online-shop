Our Heritage

A modern twist on a craft passed through generations.

Every Méiyì piece begins on the cutting table of our Kuala Lumpur atelier, where master tailors shape each panel to follow the natural line of the body. We keep the soul of the traditional qipao — the mandarin collar, the hand-knotted pankou buttons — and let go of the stiffness.

The result is a cheongsam you can wear to the office, to dinner, and to reunion — soft, structured, and unmistakably you.
