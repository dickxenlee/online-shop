Your Data, Respected

Privacy Notice

In line with the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA), Malaysia.

What we collect, and why

When you place an order we ask for your name, phone number, email and delivery address — only so we can prepare your order, deliver it, and contact you about it. If you join our newsletter, we keep your email to send you new arrivals and offers.

What we never collect

We never see or store your banking passwords or card numbers. Online payments are handled by our payment provider (toyyibPay) on their own secure pages, or arranged directly by bank transfer.

Who we share it with

Only the people needed to complete your order: our courier receives your name, phone and address for delivery, and our payment provider processes your payment. We never sell your data or share it for advertising.

Cookies

We use a small session cookie so your shopping bag and wishlist remember what you picked. No tracking cookies, no ad networks.

Your choices

You may ask us anytime to see, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you, or to stop newsletter emails. Just message us on WhatsApp or reply to any of our emails — we reply fast.

Questions about your data? We're happy to help.
