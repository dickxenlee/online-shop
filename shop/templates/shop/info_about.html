Our Story

Méiyì — 美衣, beautiful clothing

Méiyì is a women's cheongsam atelier in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia. We believe the cheongsam is not a costume for special days only — it is everyday clothing, made to move with the modern woman.

Every piece begins on our cutting table, where our tailors shape each panel to follow the natural line of the body. We keep the soul of the traditional qipao — the mandarin collar, the hand-knotted pankou buttons — and let go of the stiffness. The result is soft, structured, and unmistakably you.

From office mornings to reunion dinners, from Kuala Lumpur to Kuching — we're proud to dress Malaysian women in heritage that feels like home.
